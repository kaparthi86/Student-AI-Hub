## Full description

AI Hub starts with Student AI — a free study coach for real learning workflows.

**Ask**
Clear explanations, summaries, and study plans. Practice the topic right under the reply.

**Code**
Debug errors, understand concepts, and learn with a patient coding tutor.

**Notebook**
Upload up to 5 notes (PDF, Markdown, text, CSV, JSON) and turn them into:
- executive summaries
- key concepts
- practice checks
- a 7-day study plan
Then keep asking follow-ups grounded in your materials.

Designed for learning and practice — not for handing in AI work as your own.
Follow your instructor's AI policies and honor code.
AI Hub can make mistakes. Check important facts.

**Why students use it**
- One calm Soft paper workspace for concepts, coding help, and notes
- Practice under the topic you just studied (no separate quiz maze)
- Google sign-in
- Multilingual interface
- Non-profit framing — built to help people learn, not to sell your data or push ads
