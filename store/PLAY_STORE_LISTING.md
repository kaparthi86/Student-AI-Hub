## Full description

Student AI Hub is a free study coach built for real student workflows.

**Ask**
Get clear explanations, summaries, quizzes, and study plans.

**Code**
Debug errors, understand concepts, and learn with a patient coding tutor.

**Notebook**
Upload up to 5 notes (PDF, Markdown, text, CSV, JSON) and turn them into:
- executive summaries
- key concepts
- quizzes with answers
- a 7-day study plan
Then keep asking follow-ups grounded in your materials.

Designed for learning — not shortcuts.
Student AI Hub can make mistakes. Check important facts and follow your instructor’s AI policies and honor code.

**Why students use it**
- One calm place for concepts, coding help, and notes
- Google sign-in
- Multilingual interface
- Installable and Android-ready
