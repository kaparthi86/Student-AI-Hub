Thesis

Generic AI made answers cheap. Trust is still expensive.

People don’t need another all-purpose chatbot. They need focused help for the parts of life where mistakes matter — school, health, and money.

Problem

The current AI stack fails real life.

  • 01Students bounce between ChatGPT tabs with no study workflow, weak citations, and honor-code risk.
  • 02Health and money questions get confident, unbounded answers from tools that were never designed for trust.
  • 03Vertical apps are fragmented. Switching context means switching products, history, and brand trust.

One Hub. Specialized brains.

One identity. One calm interface. Fast expansion without rebuilding the house each time.

Each vertical gets its own tools, memory, prompts, and disclaimers — not a reskinned chat box.
