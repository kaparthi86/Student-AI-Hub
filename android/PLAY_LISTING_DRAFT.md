## Full description

My Student Coach is your personal AI study companion.

**What you can do**
- Ask — clear explanations, quizzes, and step-by-step help  
- Code — debug and learn programming with a patient tutor  
- Notebook — upload notes and get grounded study guides  
- Voice Tutor — two-way spoken Q&A (best on Chrome)  
- Personalize — board, exam focus, and language (including Hindi, Telugu, Tamil)

**Built for learning**
My Student Coach is for studying and practice. Follow your school honor code. Do not submit AI output as your own work if your course forbids it. AI can make mistakes — check important facts.

**Privacy**
Sign in with Google. We do not sell your personal data or show ads from your chats. See our Privacy Policy in the app.
