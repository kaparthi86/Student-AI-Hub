Privacy Policy

Neo Clouds Marketplace — neocloudsmarketplace.com

Last updated: September 2026

Neo Clouds is an open GPU marketplace. This policy describes what we collect when you use the site and API.

What we collect

  • Account info: name and email when you register for an API key.
  • Usage data: reservations, inference calls, and token counts for demo metering. We do not process payments.
  • Technical logs: IP address, request timestamps, and errors for security and uptime.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • We do not run third-party ad trackers on the marketplace UI.

Retention

API keys and usage records are kept while your account is active. You may request deletion by contacting the operator.

Contact

Questions: use the contact address listed on the main site or your deployment operator.
