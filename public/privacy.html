Privacy

Last updated: August 30, 2026

AI Hub helps people learn and plan with focused AI. We do not sell your personal data or show advertising based on your chats.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google through Supabase Auth, we receive basic profile details needed for login (such as email and name) from that provider.
  • Usage: messages and files you send to Student AI (Ask, Code, Notebook) or Finance AI (Ask, and numbers you choose to send from Budget or Goals) are processed to generate responses. Optional feedback (helpful / not helpful) may be stored to improve the product. Budget and goal numbers stay in your browser until you ask Finance AI to explain or plan them. Privacy Agent house rules stay in your browser. The house blocklist URL encodes only those rule choices so a DNS app can fetch the matching tracker list. If you run the optional local DNS filter, query logs stay on that computer.
  • Technical: standard server logs and rate-limit data (for example IP-based request counts) to keep the service reliable and secure.

How we use information

We use account and usage data to provide Student AI, maintain security, debug issues, and improve answer quality. AI models process your prompts to generate replies and can make mistakes — check important facts.

Processors

Sign-in is handled by Supabase. Model inference is provided via Hugging Face Inference Providers (or similar providers we configure). Hosting may be on platforms such as Render. Those services process data only to run AI Hub.

Retention

We keep account and operational data only as long as needed to run and improve the service, or as required by law. You can sign out at any time from the account menu.

Your choices

Do not submit sensitive personal information (passwords, financial account numbers, health records, or material you are not allowed to share). For account-related help, email student-ai-hub-support@googlegroups.com.

Children

AI Hub is aimed at students and adults who can create a Google account. It is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy or product feedback: student-ai-hub-support@googlegroups.com.
