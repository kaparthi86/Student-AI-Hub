Privacy

Last updated: November 13, 2026

Legal operator / controller

My Student Coach is operated by SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC (“SKM,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC is the legal operator of this website and related services, and is the organization responsible for personal information processed through My Student Coach, unless a specific feature notice says otherwise.

My Student Coach helps people learn and plan with focused AI. We do not sell your personal data or show advertising based on your chats.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google through Supabase Auth, we receive basic profile details needed for login (such as email and name) from that provider. Your learning profile preferences (level, subjects, language, region, board/exam focus) are stored in our database and linked to your account so they sync across devices.
  • Usage: messages and files you send to My Student Coach (Ask, Code, Notebook, Voice Tutor) are processed to generate responses. Conversation and notebook workspace data may be saved to your account so you can continue across devices. Optional feedback (helpful / not helpful) and Help & Support form messages (including a ticket ID) may be stored to improve the product and assist you.
  • Behavioral / product analytics (optional): when analytics are enabled in Settings, we may store product events such as feature opens (for example Ask, Notebook, Voice Tutor), session start/end, onboarding completion, profile updates, quiz or practice outcomes, and high-level learning progress signals (for example topic mastery scores). Analytics events are tied to your account ID. We do not put full chat transcripts into the analytics event stream. You can turn product usage analytics, learning analytics, and personalization off in Settings → Privacy & analytics.
  • Technical: standard server logs and rate-limit data (for example IP-based request counts) to keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Payments (if applicable): if you buy paid features, payment processors may collect billing details on behalf of SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC. SKM receives limited payment status and account information needed to fulfill the purchase.

How we use information

SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC uses account and usage data to provide My Student Coach, maintain security, debug issues, improve answer quality, personalize learning when you allow it, operate support tickets, and (when applicable) process payments. Analytics help us understand which features help students learn and where the product fails. AI models process your prompts to generate replies and can make mistakes — check important facts.

Processors

Sign-in and database hosting are handled by Supabase (including PostgreSQL storage for profiles, workspace history, analytics events, and support records). Model inference is provided via Hugging Face Inference Providers (or similar providers we configure). Hosting may be on platforms such as Render. Optional support email delivery may use Resend or SMTP. Payment processors (if enabled) handle card or wallet transactions for SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC. Those services process data only to run My Student Coach and related accounts.

Retention

We keep account, learning, and operational data only as long as needed to run and improve the service, meet customer agreement or payment obligations, or as required by law. Analytics events are retained for product improvement and typically reviewed on a rolling operational window; they are deleted with your account unless a shorter legal or security hold applies. You can sign out at any time from the account menu.

Delete account and data

Signed-in students can open More settings → Delete account & data, type DELETE, and confirm. That workflow instructs our backend to remove (or request deletion of):

  • Student profile and preferences
  • Saved conversation / workspace history we store for your account
  • Learning activity and progress records
  • Analytics events and consent records associated with your account
  • Help & Support / feedback rows linked to your user ID
  • Your authentication account with our identity provider

Deletion is enforced in backend systems (not only by hiding UI). Some backups, server logs, or payment records may remain for a limited period where required for security, fraud prevention, or law. Third-party processors are instructed or configured to delete or stop processing personal data they hold for your account where applicable. For help with deletion, use Help & Support.

Your choices

Do not submit sensitive personal information (passwords, financial account numbers, health records, or material you are not allowed to share). You may disable optional analytics and personalization in Settings. For account-related help, sign in and use Help & Support in the app. Applicable privacy rights (access, correction, deletion, or objection) can be exercised through account deletion, Settings, or Support.

Children and age policy

My Student Coach is aimed at students and adults who can create a Google account and who are 13 years of age or older. It is not directed at children under 13. We ask users to confirm this age requirement during sign-in / onboarding. Do not use the product if you are under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has created an account, contact us via Help & Support so we can delete the account and associated data.
