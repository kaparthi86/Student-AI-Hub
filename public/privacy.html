Privacy

Last updated: August 10, 2026

AI Hub is non-profit work built to help people learn and plan. We do not sell your personal data or show advertising based on your chats.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google through Supabase Auth, we receive basic profile details needed for login (such as email and name) from that provider.
  • Usage: messages and files you send to Ask, Code, or Notebook are processed to generate responses. Optional feedback (helpful / not helpful) may be stored to improve the product.
  • Technical: standard server logs and rate-limit data (for example IP-based request counts) to keep the service reliable and secure.

How we use information

We use account and usage data to provide Student AI, maintain security, debug issues, and improve answer quality. AI models process your prompts to generate replies and can make mistakes — check important facts.

Processors

Sign-in is handled by Supabase. Model inference is provided via Hugging Face Inference Providers (or similar providers we configure). Hosting may be on platforms such as Render. Those services process data only to run AI Hub.

Retention

We keep account and operational data only as long as needed to run and improve the service, or as required by law. You can sign out at any time from the account menu.

Your choices

Do not submit sensitive personal information (passwords, financial account numbers, health records, or material you are not allowed to share). To request account-related help, contact the operator of your deployment.

Children

AI Hub is aimed at students and adults who can create a Google account. It is not directed at children under 13.

Contact

Questions about this policy: use the contact channel shared with your school or cohort for this deployment of AI Hub.

This page is a plain-language privacy notice for the public launch. It is not a substitute for jurisdiction-specific legal advice.
