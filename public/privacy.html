Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 27, 2026

Student AI Hub (“we”, “our”) helps students learn with Ask, Code, and Notebook. This policy explains what data we process and why.

Information we process

  • Account data: Google sign-in identity (name, email) via Supabase Auth.
  • Study content: questions, code snippets, uploaded notes/files, and chat history needed to provide answers.
  • App preferences: language and session restore settings stored in your browser/device.
  • Technical logs: basic request metadata for reliability, security, and rate limiting.

How we use information

  • To sign you in and keep your session secure.
  • To generate study help (explanations, quizzes, study plans, code tutoring).
  • To improve reliability, prevent abuse, and fix product issues.

AI processing

Prompts and uploaded document text may be sent to our model provider to generate responses. Do not upload highly sensitive personal data. AI answers can be wrong — verify important facts and follow your school’s honor code.

Storage & retention

Chat/session restore data may be stored locally on your device. Server-side processing is used to fulfill requests. You can sign out anytime. Contact us to request account/data deletion.

Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We use service providers required to run the product (for example authentication and model inference). They process data only to provide those services.

Children

Student AI Hub is intended for students and educational use. If you are under the age required by your region to consent to online services, use the app only with a parent/guardian or school-approved account.

Your choices

  • Sign out from the account menu.
  • Disable “restore previous sessions” in Settings.
  • Clear browser/app site data to remove local history.
  • Email privacy requests to the support address listed on the Play Store listing / site footer.

Contact

Questions about privacy: use the support channel on my-student-coach.com or your Play Store listing support email.
