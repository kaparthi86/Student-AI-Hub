What we collect

How we use information

SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC uses account and usage data to provide My Student Coach, maintain security, debug issues, improve answer quality, and (when applicable) process payments and customer support. AI models process your prompts to generate replies and can make mistakes — check important facts.

Processors

Sign-in is handled by Supabase. Model inference is provided via Hugging Face Inference Providers (or similar providers we configure). Hosting may be on platforms such as Render. Payment processors (if enabled) handle card or wallet transactions for SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC. Those services process data only to run My Student Coach and related payment accounts.

Retention

We keep account and operational data only as long as needed to run and improve the service, meet customer agreement or payment obligations, or as required by law. You can sign out at any time from the account menu.

Your choices

Do not submit sensitive personal information (passwords, financial account numbers, health records, or material you are not allowed to share). For account-related help, sign in and use Help & Support in the app.
