Privacy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Legal operator / controller

My Student Coach is operated by SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC (“SKM,” “we,” “us,” or “our”). SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC is the legal operator of this website and related services, and is the organization responsible for personal information processed through My Student Coach, unless a specific feature notice says otherwise.

My Student Coach helps people learn and plan with focused AI. We do not sell your personal data or show advertising based on your chats.

What we collect

  • Account: when you sign in with Google through Supabase Auth, we receive basic profile details needed for login (such as email and name) from that provider. Your learning profile preferences (level, subjects, language, region, board/exam focus) are stored in our database and linked to your account so they sync across devices.
  • Learning content you submit: messages, code, notebook uploads, and Voice Tutor turns sent to My Student Coach are processed to generate responses. Signed-in workspace history (Ask / Code / Notebook threads and related study state) may be saved to your account in Supabase so you can continue across devices.
  • Optional feedback and support: thumbs feedback and Help & Support messages (including a ticket ID). Guest support submissions may include the contact email you provide.
  • Technical: standard server logs and rate-limit data (for example IP-based request counts) to keep the service reliable and secure.
  • Payments (if applicable): if you buy paid features, payment processors may collect billing details on behalf of SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC. SKM receives limited payment status and account information needed to fulfill the purchase.

Product analytics events (optional)

When Product usage analytics is enabled in Settings → Privacy & analytics, the app may send allowlisted behavioral events tied to your account ID. Events are limited to names such as:

  • Auth / session: login_completed, logout, session_started, session_ended
  • Navigation: tab_opened, dashboard_viewed, ai_coach_opened, progress_viewed, planner_viewed, exam_viewed
  • Learning tools: question_submitted, notebook_uploaded, notebook_analyzed, voice_session_started, voice_session_ended, flashcards_reviewed, focus_session_completed
  • Practice / journey: practice_started, practice_completed, quiz_completed, diagnostic_completed, study_plan_created, study_plan_item_completed, knowledge_gap_detected, exam_mock_completed
  • Profile / support: onboarding_completed, onboarding_skipped, profile_updated, consent_updated, support_submitted, feedback_submitted

Event properties are sanitized. Keys such as email, name, prompt, transcript, document text, and raw message content are blocked from the analytics event stream. Full chat transcripts are not written into analytics_events.

Learning analytics and personalization (optional)

  • Learning analytics: when enabled, we may store high-level learning activity and progress (for example quiz/practice counts, topic labels, mastery or gap signals) without putting full chat transcripts into those learning tables.
  • Personalization: when enabled, recommendations and home tips may use your profile and learning progress. When disabled, the product skips personalized insight calls that depend on that data.

What happens when you opt out

  • Product usage analytics off: new behavioral events are not inserted (consent_opt_out). Existing historical events remain until you delete your account (or until a retention cleanup applies).
  • Learning analytics off: new learning activity / progress writes from the learning analytics API are skipped. Product features that only need local session state may still work.
  • Personalization off: server personalization insights are not used; you may see a generic “personalization is off” state instead of tailored recommendations.

Manage these three toggles anytime in Settings → Privacy & analytics. Preferences sync to your account when you are signed in.

Voice Tutor and transcripts

Voice Tutor uses on-device browser speech recognition to capture what you say and plays coach replies with on-device speech synthesis where available. The live transcript shown in the Voice Tutor panel is kept in the browser UI for the session and is cleared when you clear the panel or leave the session.

When Voice Tutor asks the coach for a reply, your spoken turn is sent to our servers as a chat message (same AI path as Ask). That content may be included in your signed-in workspace conversation history. We do not maintain a separate long-term “voice recording” or dedicated voice-transcript database table. Voice session start/end may be recorded as analytics events (without transcript text) if product analytics is enabled.

How AI providers handle prompts and documents

To generate answers, My Student Coach sends your prompts, relevant conversation context, and (for Notebook) extracted text from uploaded documents to model inference providers we configure — currently via Hugging Face Inference Providers (or similar providers set in server configuration). Those providers process the content to return a model response. Uploaded files are parsed on our server; extracted text may be included in the model request. We do not use your chats to sell ads. Model providers may process requests under their own terms and retention practices for API inference; treat prompts and uploads as content you are comfortable sending to a cloud AI service. Always avoid passwords, financial account numbers, health records, or material you are not allowed to share.

How we use information

SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC uses account and usage data to provide My Student Coach, maintain security, debug issues, improve answer quality, personalize learning when you allow it, operate support tickets, and (when applicable) process payments. Analytics help us understand which features help students learn and where the product fails. AI models can make mistakes — check important facts.

Processors

Sign-in and database hosting are handled by Supabase (PostgreSQL for profiles, workspace history, analytics events, learning records, consent, and support/feedback rows). Model inference is provided via Hugging Face Inference Providers (or similar). Hosting may be on platforms such as Render. Optional support email delivery may use Resend or SMTP. Payment processors (if enabled) handle card or wallet transactions for SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC.

Retention

  • Account, profile, and workspace history: kept while your account is active so learning can continue across devices; removed when you complete Delete account & data (subject to short backup/log windows).
  • Analytics events and learning activity/progress: retained for product improvement while the account is active; deleted with your account. Operational reviews typically focus on recent rolling windows (for example the last 30–90 days of aggregate metrics); older raw events are not needed indefinitely for product operation.
  • Support / feedback rows: kept to resolve tickets and improve quality; deleted with your account when linked to your user ID. Guest support messages keyed only by contact email may be retained for support follow-up and then removed on a similar operational schedule.
  • Server logs / rate limits / backups: short-lived operational retention (typically days to a few weeks) for security and reliability, then rotated. Legal, fraud, or payment obligations may require longer retention of limited records.

Delete account and data

Signed-in students can open More settings → Delete account & data, type DELETE, and confirm. The backend then attempts to delete rows for your user ID in: student profile, workspace history, learning activity, learning progress, analytics events, sessions, consent, and feedback/support rows — then deletes your Supabase Auth user. Deletion verifies critical tables are empty for your ID before removing the auth account.

Client-side preferences and cached sessions on the device are cleared after a successful delete. Browser caches or offline copies on other devices may remain until those devices sign out. Third-party processors are instructed or configured to delete or stop processing personal data they hold for your account where applicable. If you cannot sign in, use the public contact form below.

Children and age policy (13+)

My Student Coach is for learners 13 years of age or older. It is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users confirm the age requirement during onboarding / an age gate. An age checkbox is a product safeguard — it does not replace applicable privacy or child-protection obligations for schools, parents, or guardians. Parents and guardians of teens should review this notice and the in-app privacy controls. If you believe a child under 13 created an account, contact us so we can delete it.

Your choices

Do not submit sensitive personal information. Disable optional analytics and personalization in Settings. Delete your account to remove stored student data as described above. Applicable privacy rights (access, correction, deletion, or objection) can be exercised through Settings, account deletion, or Contact.
