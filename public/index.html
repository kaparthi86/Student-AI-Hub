FAQ

Good questions. Clear answers.

What is My Student Coach?

A personal AI learning companion that knows what you understand, what you’re stuck on, and what to practice next — through Coach, Learning Snapshot, Practice, and Progress. Operated by SKM IT SOLUTIONS INC.

Who can use it?

Learners 13 years of age or older. Review the Privacy Policy and Terms before creating an account.

Can I upload my own study materials?

Smart Notebook accepts supported document formats so you can work with your own notes. File limits and formats apply in the product.

Can I learn with voice?

Voice Tutor lets you talk through concepts in the browser. Spoken turns sent for a coach reply follow the same path as Ask; see Privacy for transcript handling.

How do I get help or delete my data?

Use Support below (ticket ID for signed-in users). Signed-in students can delete account & data in settings. Guests can use the public support form linked from Privacy.
