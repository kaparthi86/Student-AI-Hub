# ADR-002 — Student AI only product wedge

| Field | Value |
|-------|-------|
| Status | Accepted |
| Date | 2026-11-06 |
| Deciders | Product + engineering |
| Evidence | PR #64 (Finance AI removed); earlier Hub experiments (Privacy Agent, Neo Clouds) demoted |

## Context

The Hub accumulated adjacent experiments (Finance AI, Privacy Agent, Neo Clouds). This diluted the student-study promise and increased UX/auth surface.

## Decision

Primary product is **My Student Coach / Student AI** (Ask, Code, Notebook + learning labs). Other experiments may live as separate repos or archived folders, not Hub tiles.

## Consequences

- **+** Clear story for users, investors, and Play listing  
- **+** Faster UX consistency (teal coach brand, single home)  
- **−** Abandoned short-term Finance AI usage  
- **−** Requires discipline against new hub-of-hubs ideas
